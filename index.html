FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does a small business website cost?

Most small business websites start at range from $1,500 to $8,0000 for a custom, mobile-friendly site. Our packages begin at $1,200, ranging up to only $2,500 that add local SEO, GEO, Google Business Profile setup, and integrations like booking or payments.

What's SEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps your business show up when people search on Google, the traditional list of links. GEO (generative engine optimization) helps your business get mentioned when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation. More and more customers start with AI instead of Google, so Click builds every site for both. We build to optimize clean structure, local keywords, and machine-readable business details that search engines and AI tools can understand and cite.

Do I actually own my website?

Yes, completely. We set you up so that the domain, code, and hosting all live in accounts under your name. If we ever part ways, you keep everything and your site keeps running. You're not locked in with us and welcome to work with anyone else .

How long does it take to build?

Most sites are designed, built, and live within one to three weeks, depending on how much content and how many pages are involved. Photo, video, and branding add-ons can add a little time.

Will my business show up on Google?

Every site is built with an SEO foundation. That utilize clean structure, fast load times, and local Pittsburgh keywords. Higher packages add Google Business Profile setup and ongoing optimization to help local customers find you in search and in AI tools like ChatGPT.

Do I need to be technical to manage it?

Not at all. Click builds everything for you and hand it over ready to go. If you'd rather never touch it, an optional monthly care plan lets the team handle updates, content changes, and SEO for you.

What areas do you serve?

Click is based in Pittsburgh, PA and works with small businesses across the city and surrounding areas. Everything is handled locally, where in-person meetings, shoots, and consultations are always an option.
